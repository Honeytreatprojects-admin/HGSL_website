Overview

How this division works.

Downtime is the most expensive line item on any site. Our leasing model is built around eliminating it: every unit leaves the yard on a documented service schedule, and every contract includes a maintenance response commitment rather than a best-efforts promise.

We hold a mixed fleet of earthmoving, material handling and concrete equipment sourced through authorised dealer channels, which keeps parts availability and warranty position intact. Clients range from single-site contractors to multi-year infrastructure programmes.

What we deliver

Services

Each line below is a contracted deliverable with defined scope, not a capability statement.

Dry hire

Equipment supplied to your site with condition report and handover documentation. You supply the operator; we hold the maintenance obligation.

Wet hire

Equipment plus a certified operator, fuel management and daily production reporting against agreed targets.

Project-term leasing

Fixed-fleet allocation for the life of a project, priced on duration rather than day rate, with agreed replacement units.

Maintenance and field service

Preventive schedules, mobile field response and parts sourcing for leased and client-owned fleets.

Fleet advisory

Utilisation analysis and own-versus-lease modelling for contractors sizing a fleet against a pipeline.

Logistics and mobilisation

Low-bed haulage, permitting and site delivery, including phased mobilisation on staged programmes.
