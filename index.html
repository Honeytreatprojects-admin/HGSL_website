SDI · Technology ecosystem

Our software division ships products, not proposals.

Two commercial products are live in market, built and operated in-house. Both came out of the same engineering group that runs our internal enterprise platforms.

P-01 · Healthcare technology

QwikCare

A healthcare platform that connects patients with providers and gives clinics the record, appointment and care-coordination tooling they were previously running on paper.

  • Patient records and clinical history in one place
  • Appointment booking and provider scheduling
  • Care coordination across facilities
  • Built for low-bandwidth and mobile-first access

Visit qwikcare.org

P-02 · Mobility and transportation

SurDrive

A mobility platform covering the operational layer of transport businesses — fleet, drivers, trips and the accounting that ties them together.

  • Fleet and vehicle lifecycle management
  • Driver onboarding, verification and performance
  • Trip management and dispatch
  • Revenue reconciliation and operator reporting

Visit surdrive.org

Sustainability · ESG

Commitments we report against, not slogans.

Four tracks, each with a measurable target and an owner inside the business. Progress is reviewed at board level and published annually.

Cleaner energy adoption72%

Displacing diesel and petrol with compressed natural gas across transport and industrial customers.

Responsible extraction85%

Every mining site operating under a filed rehabilitation and closure plan, with progressive restoration during production.

Local employment and skills70%

Majority local hiring at host sites, with structured skills transfer through the Honeytreat Trade Academy.

Governance and compliance90%

Documented board oversight, statutory compliance and transparent reporting across every operating entity.
